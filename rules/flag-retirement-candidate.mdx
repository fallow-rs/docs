## What it reports

Feature flag is a retirement candidate.

[Feature flags](/analysis/feature-flags) explains how fallow finds it.

## Why it matters

The flag no longer switches real behavior, but its branches stay in the code. Readers and tests keep both paths alive, and a dead path can come back on with a wrong flag value.

## Example

`FEATURE_NEW_CHECKOUT` has one read site, and `--flag-state` shows that the vendor rolled the flag out to all users.

## Fix

Confirm with the owner of the flag that the rollout is done. Remove the flag check, keep the branch that production runs, delete the other branch, then remove the flag from the vendor.
