## What it reports

Catalogue security candidate for CWE-327.

[Security](/analysis/security) explains how fallow finds it.

## Why it matters

`createCipher` derives the key without a salt and uses no random IV, so equal input gives equal output and the key is easy to brute-force. Node.js removed the function.

## Example

`crypto.createCipher('aes-256-cbc', password);`
