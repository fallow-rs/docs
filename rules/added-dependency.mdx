## What it reports

The change adds a dependency

[Changed code](/analysis/changed-code) explains how fallow finds it.

## Why it matters

`fallow audit` reports a dependency that the change adds to a `package.json`. A new dependency is often correct, so the rule defaults to `off`. Set it to `warn` or `error` to review every new package. An invented package name already fails as an unresolved import or an unlisted dependency.

## Example

A change adds `"left-pad": "^1.3.0"` to `dependencies`.

## Fix

Check that the package exists, is maintained, and is not already covered by a dependency of the project. Suppress through `overrides` because `package.json` cannot hold a comment.
