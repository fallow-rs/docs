## What it reports

Suppression comment omits a required reason.

[Suppress a finding](/guides/suppress) explains how fallow finds it.

## Why it matters

Without a reason, a reviewer cannot tell if the suppression is a decision or a shortcut. Later, nobody can check if the reason still holds, so the suppression stays forever.

## Example

`// fallow-ignore-next-line unused-export` appears without a reason after the token, while `requireSuppressionReason` is on.

## Fix

Add a concise reason after the suppression token, or remove the suppression if the issue is no longer intentional.
