## What it reports

Suppression comment omits a required reason.

[Suppress a finding](/guides/suppress) explains how fallow finds it.

## Why it matters

A fallow-ignore-next-line or fallow-ignore-file suppression omits the explanatory reason required by the requireSuppressionReason rule. Add a short reason after the suppression token, or remove the suppression if the issue is no longer intentional.

## Example

// fallow-ignore-next-line unused-export appears without the required explanatory reason.

## Fix

Add a concise reason after the suppression token, or remove the suppression if the issue is no longer intentional.
