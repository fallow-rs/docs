## What it reports

Function was invoked below the low-traffic threshold.

[Runtime coverage](/analysis/runtime-coverage) explains how fallow finds it.

## Why it matters

Production almost never runs the function, but the team still maintains and tests it. Each change to it carries risk for a path that few requests need.

## Example

`legacyLogin` ran 3 times in a capture of 2 million calls, below the `--low-traffic-threshold` fraction.

## Fix

Find the remaining callers, move them to the current path, then remove the function. Keep it when the rare callers matter.
